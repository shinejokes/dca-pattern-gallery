Part 1: Introduction & Growth Dynamics

What is DCA?

DCA (Division Cellular Automata) is a computational model that emulates the miraculous journey of a single cell growing into a complex organism.

The DNA of Patterns

Every intricate design is determined by a specific "Genome" (a sequence of numbers). Through recursive division, a simple initial square evolves into a sophisticated geometric patterns. For MSR(MultiSet based Rule scheme), genomoes are composed of 45 digits.

How it Works

  • The Rule of Division: In each time step, every cell divides into four equal daughter cells.
  • Coloring Logic: The color of the vertices and faces are determined by "Edge Rules" and "Face Rules".
  • Number of rules: 45 digits represents equal numer of rules for Muliset based(MSR) rule scheme.
  • Evolutionary Depth: Our standard gallery exhibits patterns at Time Step 10 or 11, consisting of over 1 million cells (1024 x 1024 or 2048 x 2048).

Part 2: Monochromes — The Skeleton of DCA

By stripping away color, we reveal the fundamental Skeletal Structure of Division Cellular Automata.

  • Pure Geometry: By excluding Face Color #4, we explore the aesthetic of raw boundaries and the negative space created by cellular division.
  • Recursive Complexity: Observe the extreme precision of 10-step recursive growth within the minimalist harmony of black and white.
